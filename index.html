Spacing & enclosure

Why these spaces exist — the standards behind them
Cell spacing is not padding: prismatic and pouch cells SWELL 10–20% in thickness over life (the design allowance under constraint — unconstrained cells measure far worse), and the gap doubles as the thermal break that propagation tests (IEC 62619 §8, GB 38031) probe. A spacer from the Components tab sets this number from a real part.

Wall thickness is what the crash and crush tests demand: no standard prescribes a millimetre value — UN ECE R100 Annex 4, GB 38031 and UL 2580 prescribe TESTS (mechanical shock, crush, integrity) the wall must pass for your market. The slider reserves the space; the proof is the test.

Busbar/BMS headroom keeps room above the cells for interconnects, sense wiring and the gas path to the vent — the sources give no vent-area sizing method (marked honestly, not guessed), but a blocked vent path fails every abuse test.
